A simple routine

Four steps to sort smarter

A few careful habits can make waste easier to handle and improve the chance that recyclable materials are accepted.

①

Identify

Check what the item is made from and whether it has mixed materials.

②

Empty & clean

Empty containers and rinse food residue when required by local rules.

③

Separate

Keep food scraps, recyclables, general waste, and special waste apart.

④

Check locally

Follow your municipality's accepted materials and collection instructions.

Good habits, every day

Little changes add up

Start with practical steps that fit your routine. Reuse first, recycle where accepted, and handle special waste safely.

🚰

Carry reusables

Keep a refillable bottle and reusable bag handy to reduce single-use items.

🥬

Plan food wisely

Plan meals, store food well, and compost suitable scraps where a service is available.

🔋

Handle special waste

Keep batteries and electronics out of ordinary bins; use approved collection points.
